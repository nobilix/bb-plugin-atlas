## Arbitration: six, five, thirty-two, thirty-seven

Checked at `7b26cae` (0.49.0): `interface PluginAppBuilder` has **exactly six members** — `slots`, `commands`, `composer`, `contentScripts`, `experimental_icons`, `experimental_sidebarFooter`. `interface PluginAppSlots` has **32 method names** (22 at 0.43.3).

Both earlier numbers were right about different things, so the site fixes the vocabulary once:

- **`PluginAppBuilder` has six members.** Prose may say "the builder gives you six registration surfaces" — that is correct and stays.
- **One of the six is `slots`**, the container for the 32 slot methods.
- **The other five are builder regions**, which is what `data/slots.json` counts beside the methods.
- **37 registration points** = 32 slot methods + 5 builder regions. Never a summed "slots" number, never "six builder regions".

Where a page prints counts, read them off the data and never sum them into a single unlabelled number.
